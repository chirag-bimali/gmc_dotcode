About the Event

Gear up for the most anticipated coding challenge of the semester! The Winter Hackathon 2024 is a 48-hour intensive building experience where students from all disciplines come together to solve real-world problems using technology.

Work in teams of up to 4 to develop innovative prototypes, receive mentorship from industry professionals, and compete for over $5,000 in prizes.
